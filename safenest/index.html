Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does SafeNest charge? ▼
The initial consultation is always free. Our fees vary based on the scope of services needed. In many cases, our involvement pays for itself many times over by recovering fair market value on assets. We discuss all fees transparently before any engagement.
What if the auction has already happened? ▼
It's not always too late. If you believe your family was taken advantage of, contact us immediately. We can review auction records, compare sale prices to market values, and advise on potential next steps. Time matters, so don't wait.
What areas do you serve? ▼
We are based in Champion, Pennsylvania and primarily serve Western and Central Pennsylvania, including Pittsburgh, Johnstown, Somerset, Uniontown, and surrounding areas. We can also consult remotely for families in other states.
How quickly can you respond to an urgent situation? ▼
For urgent cases — such as an auction scheduled within days — we respond within hours. Contact us immediately and explain the timeline. We will do everything possible to be there when you need us.
Do you work with attorneys or financial advisors? ▼
Yes. We frequently work alongside estate attorneys, financial advisors, and social workers to provide comprehensive protection. We can also refer you to trusted professionals in our network if needed.
