How PawCoins Works

A simple, transparent reward loop that puts money back in your pocket for being a responsible pet owner.

01
🐶

Care For Your Pet

Take your pet to the vet, buy food at a pet store, get grooming services — every verified pet care action qualifies.

02
📱

Log Your Activity

Upload your receipt or vet record through the PawCoins app. Our system verifies the transaction automatically.

03
🪙

Earn $PAWC Tokens

Verified activities are rewarded with $PAWC tokens deposited directly to your Solana wallet. The more you care, the more you earn.

04
💰

Convert to Real Money

$PAWC tokens can be held, traded, or converted to SOL and then to USD through any major crypto exchange.
